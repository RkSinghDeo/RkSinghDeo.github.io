The Situation

A data team running batch ETL every 4 hours. On paper that sounds fine — until a fraud signal surfaces at 11pm and the analyst only sees it at 3am when the next batch runs. Payment failures, inventory depletion, and anomalies all landed in the warehouse four hours late.

The existing pipeline was an S3 dump → Glue job → Redshift pattern. Solid for reporting, totally wrong for anything time-sensitive. Duplicate records on retry were silently dropped or double-counted. There were no consumer lag alarms. Failures were discovered by analysts, not systems.

The ask: replace the batch window with a streaming pipeline that surfaces events within 30 seconds, handles at-least-once Kinesis delivery safely, and keeps Iceberg tables consistent.

What I Built

Architecture
graph LR PROD["Event Producers API · Mobile · IoT"] --> KDS["Kinesis Data Streams 4 shards · 24h retention"] KDS --> LAMBDA["Lambda Consumer batch 100 · parallelization 2"] LAMBDA --> DLQ["SQS DLQ invalid records"] KDS --> GLUE["Glue Streaming PySpark micro-batch every 60s"] GLUE -->|"MERGE INTO CDC"| ICE[("Apache Iceberg S3 partitioned by event_date")] ICE --> ATH["Amazon Athena real-time SQL"] CW["CloudWatch IteratorAge alarm"] --> ALERT["Alert on lag > 60s"]
  • Kinesis Data Streams (4 shards): 4 MB/s ingestion, 24-hour retention for safe replay. Shard count sized at 4× peak expected throughput to leave headroom for traffic spikes.
  • Lambda consumer (validation layer): Reads from Kinesis in batches of 100. Validates schema, enriches partition keys, routes invalid records to SQS DLQ with full payload — no silent drops.
  • Glue Streaming PySpark job: 60-second micro-batches. Parses JSON from Kinesis, casts types, filters nulls, deduplicates on event_id within each micro-batch.
  • Apache Iceberg MERGE INTO: Upserts on event_id make the pipeline idempotent — safe to replay any micro-batch without double-counting. Kinesis's at-least-once guarantee becomes exactly-once in storage.
  • CloudWatch IteratorAge alarm: Fires if consumer lag exceeds 60 seconds. Catches throughput spikes or job stalls before analysts notice missing data.
Event Producers (API / Mobile / IoT)
