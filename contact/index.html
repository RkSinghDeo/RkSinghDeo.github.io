Let’s talk about your project#

The fastest way to get started is through Upwork — that’s where I handle contracts and payments.

Before reaching out, it helps to have a rough sense of:

  • What the current situation is (what’s broken, slow, or expensive)
  • What “done” looks like for you
  • Your rough timeline

You don’t need a technical spec — I’ll ask the right questions.

What happens after you message me#

  1. I reply within 24 hours (usually faster) to ask a few clarifying questions.
  2. If it’s a good fit, I send a fixed-scope proposal: deliverables, timeline, and price. No hourly ambiguity.
  3. We agree on scope, I start within the week.
