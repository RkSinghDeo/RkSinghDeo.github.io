Services
  • FinOps Audit

    Most AWS teams overspend by 25–40% on compute they provisioned once and never revisited. I find exactly where your Lambda and Glue budget is leaking, hand you a prioritised list of fixes with ready-to-apply config patches, and leave a monitoring stack so the bill doesn't quietly drift back up after the engagement ends.

    1 week
  • Serverless ETL Pipeline

    If your team finds out a pipeline failed when an analyst complains the next morning, that's a fixable problem, not a fact of life. I build pipelines that validate data before it reaches your warehouse, surface failures within minutes, and replay cleanly without manual intervention. Observability and alerting ship as part of the build, not bolted on later.

    2–3 weeks
  • Lakehouse Migration

    Slow queries, schema changes that require a freeze window, and tables nobody dares touch are architecture problems, not Glue problems. I move your data to a modern lakehouse structure, run your old and new systems in parallel until everything reconciles, then cut over automatically with zero downtime. Your team inherits infrastructure they can actually evolve.

    3–4 weeks
  • AI Data Agent

    When business users can't query their own data without filing a ticket, the data team becomes a bottleneck. I build AI agents that let anyone ask questions in plain English and get answers directly from your warehouse, no SQL, no BI tool, no queue. Every agent is scoped to approved data only, blocks write operations at the architecture level, and is fully owned by your team from day one.

    1–2 weeks
