01 / The Brief

The Brief

A financial services data team was spending 3-4 hours per week manually querying Athena, compiling Lambda utilization stats, and writing cost summaries for engineering leadership. The brief was to fully automate the audit-to-summary pipeline and surface insights in plain English every morning without adding headcount.

02 / What Was Delivered

What Was Delivered

Architecture
graph LR EB["EventBridge 03:00 UTC"] --> LM["Lambda bedrock-insights"] LM --> S3R[("S3 Right-Sizing Reports")] LM --> S3C[("S3 Cost Explorer Data")] S3R --> BR["Amazon Bedrock Claude 3.5 Sonnet"] S3C --> BR BR -->|"plain-English summary"| S3O[("S3 bedrock-insights/ YYYY-MM-DD")]
  • Nightly Lambda (03:00 UTC) reads right-sizing reports and Cost Explorer data from S3
  • Calls Claude 3.5 Sonnet via Amazon Bedrock with a structured FinOps prompt
  • Generates a 400-word plain-English executive summary with prioritized actions
  • Saves report to S3 under bedrock-insights/YYYY-MM-DD/ with JSON metadata
  • CDK-deployed with scoped IAM (bedrock:InvokeModel + S3 read) and EventBridge schedule
