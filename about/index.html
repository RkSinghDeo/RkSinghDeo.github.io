Who I am#

I’m Ramneek Singh — an AWS Data Engineer who builds serverless data pipelines, lakehouse architectures, and cost-optimized ETL systems.

For the past 5 years I’ve shipped production AWS data infrastructure for companies ranging from early-stage SaaS to Fortune 500 enterprises. The work that matters most to me: taking a data team from “our pipelines keep breaking” to “we can ship new features because the platform just works.”

What I’m good at#

  • Serverless-first architecture — Lambda, Glue, Step Functions, SQS, EventBridge. I’ve replaced dozens of always-on servers with event-driven pipelines that cost pennies at idle and scale automatically.
  • Apache Iceberg at scale — migration from Hive-partitioned tables, MERGE INTO CDC, schema evolution, partition pruning optimization, Glue Data Catalog as Iceberg REST catalog.
  • AWS cost engineering — I’ve delivered FinOps audits that found $33K+/year in waste. Lambda Power Tuning, Glue auto-scaling, S3 Intelligent Tiering, Athena workgroup cost controls.
  • CDK v2 in Python — every project I deliver includes reproducible IaC. Your infrastructure is version-controlled, testable, and deployable by your team without me.
  • Data quality that actually ships — Great Expectations, custom CloudWatch metrics, dbt tests. Quality gates that block bad data before it reaches your analysts, not after.

Tech stack I use daily#

Compute: AWS Lambda · AWS Glue 4.0 (PySpark) · Amazon MWAA (Airflow) · AWS Step Functions
Storage: Amazon S3 · Apache Iceberg · Amazon DynamoDB · Amazon Redshift Serverless
