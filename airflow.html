The Situation

An e-commerce company with a cron-triggered Lambda chain tied together by SNS. Silent failures. No retry logic. Engineers found out a pipeline had failed when analysts showed up at 9am asking why the dashboard hadn't updated.

Three to four SLA misses per month. On-call engineers spending Sunday mornings replaying jobs by hand pulling logs, figuring out where it broke, manually triggering re-runs, and praying it didn't miss the next window. 600K records per week moving through a system with no observability.

They needed real orchestration. Not another Lambda chain with better error handling actual DAG-based orchestration with sensors, retries, alerting, and one-click replay.

What I Built

Architecture
graph LR S3["S3 Raw Data"] -->|"ObjectCreated"| SQS["SQS Queue"] SQS --> MWAA["MWAA Airflow DAG SqsSensor"] MWAA --> GLUE["Glue PySpark Transform"] GLUE -->|"Silver Iceberg"| VALID["Lambda Validator"] VALID -->|"pass"| RS["Redshift COPY"] VALID -->|"fail"| ALERT["Slack Alert + halt"] RS --> CW["CloudWatch + Alerts"]
  • SqsSensor in reschedule mode: Waits for source files to land in S3 (via SQS notification). No idle Lambda cost. Triggers only when data is actually there.
  • GlueJobOperator: PySpark transform with job bookmarking. Idempotent by design safe to replay without producing duplicates.
  • Lambda data validator: Record count check + null rate must be below 1%. Publishes a CloudWatch custom metric (ETLPipeline/DataQuality). Pipeline halts and alerts if validation fails.
  • RedshiftSQLOperator: COPY from validated Silver S3 prefix into Redshift Serverless. Only runs if validation passes.
  • Slack notifications: On success and failure with record count, duration, and DPU consumed. On-call engineers see the result on their phone before they open their laptop.
  • CloudWatch dashboard: Record counts, job duration, DPU utilization, and data quality score in one view. No more guessing.
